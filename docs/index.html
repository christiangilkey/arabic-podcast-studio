Arabic Podcast Studio

Learn Arabic from real podcasts, one word at a time.

A free, open-source app for studying Arabic podcasts. Subscribe to any podcast by its RSS feed and transcribe episodes on your own computer with OpenAI's Whisper. Then read along as each word lights up while it's spoken.

  • Word-level transcripts in Modern Standard Arabic and dialects, made locally. Your audio never leaves your computer.
  • Tap any word to hear just that word and see what it means in that sentence, using your own AI provider key (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or Grok).
  • Slow playback, sentence looping and auto-scrolling right-to-left text.
  • Vocab list that saves each word with its sentence and audio, and exports to Anki.
  • Optional sync between your computers and phone through a private folder in your own Google Drive.

Download for Windows, macOS or Linux
