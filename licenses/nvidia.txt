NVIDIA cuBLAS and cuDNN (optional GPU acceleration download)
==========================================================

These libraries are NOT included in the installer. If you choose "Install GPU
acceleration" in Settings, the app downloads NVIDIA's official redistributable packages
(nvidia-cublas-cu12 and nvidia-cudnn-cu12) directly from the Python Package Index
(https://pypi.org/) and verifies their SHA-256 checksums.
